About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web application designed to help you capture your ideas instantly before they slip away. Whether you're brainstorming, studying, or planning, QuickNotes provides a clean and fast way to jot down your thoughts.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note in the text box under "Add a note"
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save it
  3. View all your notes in the "Your notes" section below

Features

  • Fast and simple note-taking
  • Clean, distraction-free interface
  • Works offline in your browser
  • No account required

Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + Enter Add a new note
Escape Clear the input field
